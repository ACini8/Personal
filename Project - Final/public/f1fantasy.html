F1 Fantasy

What is F1 Fantasy?

F1 Fantasy is a game available on the Formula 1 app, which allows you to pick 5 drivers and 2 teams with a budget of 100 million euros. Depending on how these drivers do in a Grand Prix weekend, you will get points based on their performance.

Last year over 1.5 million users took part, and the overall winner gets prizes such as 2x Paddock Pass Weekend Tickets to any Grand Prix of the winner's choosing! You can create mini leagues with your friends and compete with one another, or you can join the TGPN Mini-League, where the winner will get a £50 gift card to the F1 gift shop!
